# PAA V0.10 Pilot Gates

These gates define when the build is ready to move from creator-led testing toward a broader pilot. They are product gates, not claims of psychological or predictive validity.

## Gate A — Build integrity
- clean checkout installs without manual source rearrangement;
- `npm run typecheck` passes;
- Expo public config resolves;
- iOS and Android preview builds launch;
- no known blocker crash in the guided core flow.

## Gate B — First-time comprehension
Test at least 5 people who have not been taught the PAA operator system beforehand.
- they can explain, in their own words, the distinction between numerical hypothesis and behavioral evidence;
- no recurring confusion prevents completion;
- operator labels remain hidden during BOI response selection.

## Gate C — UAT cohort
Run an initial cohort of at least 15–30 independent testers before redesigning the core instrument around isolated reactions.
Track separately:
- assessment completion;
- final-survey completion;
- comprehension;
- result fit;
- usefulness;
- method trust;
- return intent;
- recurring qualitative friction;
- bugs.

## Gate D — Instrument scrutiny
Before any claim of validated behavioral measurement:
- estimate internal consistency only where statistically appropriate;
- perform test–retest work;
- inspect operator confusion patterns;
- test whether scenario wording or answer position drives outcomes;
- define external behavioral criteria;
- preregister prospective hypotheses where feasible;
- compare against relevant baselines/chance models.

## Gate E — Institutional / licensing readiness
Before licensing or sale to institutions:
- formalize ownership and licensing terms for PAA canon, content, software, and datasets;
- produce privacy policy, data retention/deletion policy, security review, and store disclosures;
- define prohibited uses, especially clinical diagnosis, high-stakes employment decisions, and unsupported deterministic profiling;
- document versioned scoring rules and change control;
- provide a reproducible data dictionary and pilot report;
- separate commercial claims from research hypotheses.

## Release principle
A breakthrough product is not one that cannot be contradicted. It is one whose architecture makes contradiction visible, preservable, and useful.
