# PAA V0.10 Release Notes

V0.10 is the first build designed explicitly for a repeatable real-person UAT cohort rather than creator-led demonstration.

## What changed for testers
- clearer V0.10 guided UAT state on the home screen;
- blinded BOI answer choices remain intact;
- completed readings route into a short final product survey;
- survey ratings do not alter the PAA result;
- testers can report specific problems separately from overall ratings.

## What changed for researchers
- product acceptance and model evidence are displayed separately;
- structured metrics: completion, survey return, comprehension, result fit, usefulness, trust in method, and return intent;
- shared-device reset removes the prior tester's current state/profile while preserving historical cohort records;
- UAT export includes session, issue-feedback, and final-survey records;
- data dictionary and pilot gates make the cohort auditable by an outside reviewer.

## What V0.10 does not claim
V0.10 does not establish psychological validity, deterministic number-to-person causation, clinical utility, or suitability for high-stakes profiling. Its purpose is to produce cleaner evidence about usability, interpretive value, operator discrimination, and prospective performance.

## Recommended initial cohort
Begin with 5 naive testers for comprehension debugging. Freeze wording after blocker fixes, then run 15–30 testers without teaching the operator key before BOI completion. Export the dataset before changing the core instrument again.
